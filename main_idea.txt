LIFELOG AI: The Multi-Sensory Cognitive Partner

CORE IDENTITY

LIFELOG AI is a Unified Perception Engine. It bridges the gap between what you do and what you say, acting as a "Silent Witness" that provides proactive help, infinite recall, and behavioral insights. It transforms your environment into an Actionable Database.

HOW IT WORKS (The "Fusion" Intelligence Layer)

A. Audio-Visual Temporal Fusion

The AI doesn't process sight and sound separately; it fuses them into a single "Chronicle."

The Process: It aligns a video keyframe of you holding a document with the audio of you saying, "I’ll put this receipt here for the tax audit."

The Result: Instead of a generic log ("User put paper on desk"), it creates a high-context event: [14:22] User filed Tax Receipt (2025) on the corner of the mahogany desk.

B. Intent & Commitment Extraction

The mic doesn't just record; it parses.

Fact Extraction: "I'm hiding the spare key under the blue pot." -> The AI updates its "Object State" database instantly.

Commitment Tracking: "Remind me to call the plumber when I get home." -> The AI identifies the intent and triggers a "Welcome Home" alert when it sees you re-enter the front door.

C. Causal Knowledge Graph (The "Reasoning" Brain)

Standard AI uses simple lists. LIFELOG AI builds a Dynamic Graph of your life.

Nodes: People, Objects, Places, Concepts (e.g., "Taxes," "Vacation").

Edges: Actions and Relationships (e.g., "User stored Passport in Safe").

This allows for Multi-hop Reasoning: If you ask, "Where is the thing I was holding when I was talking about the Japan trip?", the AI traverses the graph from the concept "Japan" to the specific audio-visual event.

KEY INNOVATIONS

Proactive "Intent Correction": If the AI hears you say, "I'm going to the gym," but sees you walking toward the door without your gym bag, it provides a voice nudge: "Don't forget your gym bag; it's still in the laundry room."

Ambient Safety Guard: It recognizes the sound of a "boiling pot" combined with the visual of an empty kitchen. Action: It sends an urgent alert to your phone: "The kitchen is unattended and I hear a pot boiling over."

Semantic "Vibe" Search: You can query abstract feelings or trends. "How many hours did I actually spend focused this week?" "When did I last feel stressed? (AI analyzes audio tone + posture)."
